# Social compliance

Social content is text drafts only.

- No guaranteed approval, no "lowest rates", no invented statistics.
- Use only rates the user supplied, plus a reminder to add the required disclosure.
- Equal Housing and NMLS placeholders unless the real values are in the CRM.
- No discriminatory targeting; no fake testimonials; no personal data from the CRM; no unproven competitor claims.
- X posts are at most 280 characters.
